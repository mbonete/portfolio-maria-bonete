Keeping coin purchases from being lost or granted twice

20 November 2026 · 3 min read · Marine Wobbles

Each purchase is recorded twice, in Google's records and in the game's save file, and the two can drift apart. These are the five rules the game follows to keep them matched.

THE FIVE RULES

  1. 1Grant coins only for paid purchases
  2. 2Save the coins before consuming
  3. 3Grant each purchase token once
  4. 4Check for unfinished purchases on every return
  5. 5Keep the game usable when Google Play can't be reached
1

Grant coins only for paid purchases

Google marks a purchase as PURCHASED or PENDING. Coins are granted only for PURCHASED. While a slow payment such as cash at a shop is pending, the pack shows “Waiting for payment — your coins arrive when Google confirms it.”
