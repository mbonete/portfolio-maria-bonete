# Portfolio

Product context, audience and content constraints (no job-seeking, nothing fabricated, AI-section privacy): `PRODUCT.md`.

## Writing the portfolio's copy

Applies to everything a visitor or crawler reads: page text, case studies, metadata, alt text, link labels. Use it when writing, rewriting or reviewing copy.

Write like the developer who did the work, explaining it to a peer: plain, specific, first person where it was her decision. Vary sentence length and shape. The story is the real problem, the decision, the trade-off and what happened; let those carry the interest.

Avoid:
- Contrast for effect: "X, not Y", "it's not just X", "more than a…". Keep a contrast only when two real options were compared (device vs server checks, two briefs in an A/B test).
- Slogans, teasers and aphorisms: "without the mess", "the interesting part is underneath", "the order of operations is the design".
- Marketing words: seamless, robust, innovative, cutting-edge, leverage, solutions, passionate, journey, experiences (meaning products).
- Rhetorical questions, dramatic openings, metaphors that stand in for a fact.
- Claims with no evidence on the page: "fast", "intuitive", "proven".
- Saying a fact twice. A summary, a facts list and highlights placed together must each add something new.

Keep:
- Numbers, names of techniques and the reasons behind decisions. Cut words, not technical detail.
- Every fact traceable to the repos, the CV or `PRODUCT.md`. Never invent achievements, metrics, motivations or experience; if wording needs a fact you don't have, ask.

Before finishing, reread the changed copy against the list above and fix what you find. Also check that no sentence could be deleted without losing a fact or a reason.
