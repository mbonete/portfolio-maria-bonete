# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers filling full-stack, mobile (React Native / native Android), Web3 and AI-engineering roles. They often arrive from LinkedIn on a phone, between other candidates. Their first job is a ten-second decision: shortlist or move on. Their second job, once interested, is to check the proof: shipped work, roles, and how she works.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Maria Bonete, a developer based in Madrid, Spain, with a BA in Art History (Complutense University of Madrid, Extraordinary Award 9.48/10, Excellence Scholarship, 17 honours) who moved into software. It exists to get her shortlisted. Success is a visitor contacting her, downloading the CV, or opening LinkedIn/GitHub.

## Positioning

A developer who ships across four areas with equal weight: full-stack web, mobile (React Native and native Android), Web3, and AI-driven engineering. AI knowledge must be clear from the very first viewport, presented as a peer of the other three, not an add-on. Her AI angle is rigour, not enthusiasm: she runs agents with measured experiments, independent verification, and real results, and treats agent output as a claim to verify.

## Operating Context

Visitors skim on phones and laptops, often in a recruiting pipeline with many tabs open. Deeper visitors (engineering managers) open case studies, the CV PDF, and GitHub repos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind 4; deployed as a static-friendly site.
- Sections: Hero, Projects, AI (stance, principles with measured results, toolkit), Experience, About, Contact, plus a "How this site was built" page.
- Features in scope: ⌘K command palette, case-study pages with view transitions, a "now" strip (latest public GitHub push, Madrid time, a hand-edited "currently" line), WebGL where it earns its place.
- Mobile: LCP under 2.5s on a mid-range phone; heavy effects degrade on small screens and under reduced motion.
- Free fonts only.

## Brand Commitments

- Design reference, not content: her taste is Bosch's *The Garden of Earthly Delights* (her desktop wallpaper), Japanese and Chinese art, and Art Nouveau; her own work leans to glossy inflated 3D, flowing lines and purple. Her hobbies (photography, travel, art, books, music) inform the design but are not shown as site content.
- Do not mention job-seeking. Use only photos of Maria alone or of places.

- Dark only.
- Real photo of Maria (public/lab/maria.png, cutout at public/lab/maria-cutout.png). Exception chosen by the owner: the Gold Screen hero shadow uses an owner-supplied profile silhouette that is AI-generated (its C2PA marks say so); its provenance record states that.
- Must not look like a generic AI-generated site.
- Learning material behind the AI section is never credited or presented as formal study; its wording, prices, model opinions and unfinished material stay private, and no private project is named. AI results are described generically.

## Product Principles

1. Ten seconds to "shortlist": role, four pillars and a way to act are legible in the first viewport on a phone.
2. Proof over claims: every assertion links to shipped work, a role, or a measured result.
3. The site itself is evidence of craft and of her AI workflow.
4. Honest status: unfinished work is labelled as such.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion; keyboard-operable command palette and navigation; legible contrast on the dark ground.
